# Build 327 round 7 (b327y) - no hard line round a circle

Madison (Oct 22): "some of the circles shaped stuff across the app, especially
the Home Screen has like a hard but very hard to notice line at the bottom and
maybe the top ... some of the flares have a line at the bottom of the bubble
that makes it look weird. The daily question button is the same."

## What it was (measured at 3x down each one's centre column)
1. A background image repeats under a see-through border. The flare bubbles
   (`.cosmic-icon-badge`), the daily question button, the chat button and
   Pause all lay a top-lit gradient over their fill and have a 1px
   translucent border. The image is sized to the padding box and repeats
   under the border, so the strip under the BOTTOM border showed the
   gradient's TOP (its brightest highlight): a 3-device-px bright band round
   the bottom rim (an unlit bubble's bottom rim 120 -> 162 of 255; the daily
   button's 56 -> 73), and the gradient's bottom under the top border.
2. The chat button and Pause wore `--surface-shadow`, whose catch-light is
   `inset 0 1px 0` and lip `inset 0 -1px 1px`: hairlines ruled round the top
   and bottom of a circle.
3. The bubble's rim glow (`::before`) started 1px inside the border, leaving
   a dark hairline ring between the rim and the glow.

## What changed (one CSS block, "NO HARD LINE ROUND A CIRCLE")
- `background-repeat:no-repeat; background-origin:border-box` (!important,
  because later rules set the `background` shorthand) on
  `.cosmic-icon-badge`, `.daily-question-fab`, `.chatdock-btn` and Pause.
  The gradient starts at the outer edge, is painted once, and fades out at
  the rim.
- The bubble `::before` starts at the rim (`inset:0`) with a slightly softer
  glow and a faint even feather.
- The daily button gets a faint even inner feather (`inset 0 0 4px`).
- The chat button and Pause take the same light softened: catch-light and
  lip are `inset 0 +-3px 5px -2px`. `--surface-shadow` itself is untouched
  (it is on dozens of cards, where a hairline reads as an edge).
- Sizes, positions, colours, lit/unlit tints and the daily ring unchanged.
