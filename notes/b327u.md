## What changed
1. The status line under the question (`.qpanel > .status`) changes on the
   choice colour's own timing: `statusWordsIn()` (called in render()) fades the
   new words in over STATUS_SWAP_MS 360ms on `cubic-bezier(.25,.6,.3,1)`, no
   delay - the same .36s curve the picked choice's colour eases in on - while
   the old words fade out from a `::before` (data-was), laid over, so the
   line's height never jumps and textContent is only the new words. A render
   that does not change the words (or a new question) plays nothing. Reduce
   motion: no fade. `statusShown` is `var` (render() can run before its line).
2. The rank-up climb on Profile (`rankUpStandingClimb`) has no "N extra XP".
   The line under the bar is the ordinary "x / y XP"; at the start x is the
   whole XP into the wall level, larger than y, the bar full. Each level: the
   level up plays (the bar pops - `.ru-levelup` with rs-trackpop - the end
   flash, `res.levelup`), the level number steps on, the bar empties and x
   counts DOWN by what that level cost while the bar fills from what is left
   (the remainder over the NEW level's span). Ends on the remainder, or full
   at the next wall. `buildXpExtra` and `.pf-xp-extra*` are deleted.
   `scene.climb.steps` gains `prev` (x before the level's cost came off); sum
   = xpForLevel(level) + have = total at every step.
3. The completed challenge card fades into the next rank's - RU_HERO_FADE_MS
   (900ms) ease-in-out, opacity only, rankUpHeroTurn's fade - with the old
   card laid over the new at the same box (`.ru-goal-old`) and removed after.
   No rotate, no perspective; the flip CSS (.ru-flipping, ru-flip-out/in) is
   gone. The new card's pieces still come in one at a time (ru-goal-pre/in).
