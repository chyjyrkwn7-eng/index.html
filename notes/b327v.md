## The road map
What "skips sections" was, measured:
1. Every reached stretch was refilled from 0 on EVERY open of the Rank tab,
   all at once (`fillsToAnimate` in `buildRanksTab`): the line appeared in
   several pieces down the map at the same time.
2. The rank-up show on the map opened the circle (`is-celebrating`) 1.5-1.8s
   into a fill that takes 2.65s (scale ~.95 measured), then `finish()` set
   --lit 1 and the last stretch jumped.
3. A climb from nothing faded its 9px stub in (opacity, .25s late) beside a
   line already running: a gap that appeared all at once.
4. The journey's ride (`rankUpRide`) could compute k < 0 on a frame stamped
   before it began.

Fixes:
- A stretch that did not move since you last looked is set at build time
  (no transition), its stub shown, its light placed instantly
  (`--tip-still`). Only the climb and the celebration animate.
- The celebration waits for the fill's own `transitionend` (2.9s cap).
- The stub grows out of the circle (`transform:scaleY`, .22s linear) and the
  climb's line sets off from its end 230ms later.
- `rankUpRide` clamps k to [0, 1].
